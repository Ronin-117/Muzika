Install Muzika on Any Device

Muzika is a Progressive Web App (PWA). It doesn't require downloading through crowded app stores. Follow the simple steps below to add it directly to your home screen with its custom icon.

IOS SAFARI STEP-BY-STEP Takes less than 15 seconds
  1. 1

    Open in Safari

    Navigate to https://my-muzika.duckdns.org/ inside Apple Safari on your iPhone or iPad.

  2. 2

    Tap the Safari Share Button

    At the bottom toolbar of Safari, tap the Share icon (the square box with an upward-pointing arrow).

  3. 3

    Select "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down the sharing sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.

  4. 4

    Verify "Open as Web App" & Tap Add

    In iOS 17+, make sure the Open as Web App toggle is enabled so Muzika uses its standalone icon, custom splash screen, and full native screen estate. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

You're all set! Muzika will now appear right on your home screen with background audio and lock screen widget support.
ANDROID CHROME / BRAVE One-tap native APK-like installation
  1. 1

    Open in Chrome or Brave

    Visit https://my-muzika.duckdns.org/.

  2. 2

    Tap "Install" Banner or Menu (⋮)

    A prompt will automatically appear at the bottom asking to Add Muzika to Home screen. Alternatively, tap the 3 dots in the top-right corner of Chrome and select Install app.

  3. 3

    Confirm Installation

    Tap Install. Muzika will install as a native standalone PWA inside your app drawer with notification center and Bluetooth car media controls.

WINDOWS / MAC / LINUX Desktop window with media keys
  1. 1

    Open in Chrome, Edge, or Brave

    Visit https://my-muzika.duckdns.org/.

  2. 2

    Click the Install Icon in the Address Bar

    Look at the right side of the address bar for the small computer/download icon labelled Install Muzika.

  3. 3

    Launch from Desktop or Dock

    Muzika opens in its own borderless window. Keyboard play/pause media keys work seamlessly.
